  • Does Rural E-Commerce Reduce Street Crime? Evidence From Taobao Villages in China

    Yunze Han, Qing Sun*, and Kei Kajisa

    Bulletin of Economic Research · Early View · 20 October 2026

    Abstract

    Rural e‐commerce development is believed to generate positive economic effects, but little research examines its other social effects. We study whether the emergence of Taobao villages reduces property‐related street and neighborhood crime at the county level in China. Using a panel of 2,872 counties from 2014 to 2019, we estimate this effect using a difference‐in‐differences approach. The results show that counties with Taobao villages experience a significant decline in property‐related street and neighborhood crime. By showing that Taobao villages may also affect local public safety, these findings extend the literature on rural e‐commerce beyond its traditional focus on economic outcomes to broader social outcomes.

  • The effect of government expenditure and comparative advantage on agricultural green development in OECD countries

    Qing Sun* and Shinichi Kitano

    Journal of Environmental Information Science · 2026, vol. 2026, no. 1, pp. 25–36

    Abstract

    This study examines how revealed comparative advantage (RCA) and agricultural government expenditure are associated with standard total factor productivity (STFP) and green total factor productivity (GTFP) in OECD countries from 2005 to 2021. In measuring GTFP, we account for both negative externalities from carbon emissions and positive externalities from soil carbon sequestration. Using dynamic panel analysis, we find that RCA is positively associated with both STFP and GTFP, and that agricultural government expenditure is related to both productivity measures. However, the estimated association of government expenditure is larger for STFP than for GTFP. Overall, the findings highlight the importance of considering both environmental costs and positive environmental externalities when evaluating the role of government support and agricultural competitiveness in sustainable agricultural development.
