  • Does Rural E-Commerce Reduce Street Crime? Evidence From Taobao Villages in China

    Yunze Han, Qing Sun, and Kei Kajisa

    Bulletin of Economic Research · Early View · 20 October 2026

    Abstract

    How does the emergence of a Taobao village change public safety in the surrounding county? We use a difference-in-differences design to study 2,872 Chinese counties over 2014–2019. Property crime on streets and in residential neighborhoods fell significantly in counties where Taobao villages emerged. The findings suggest that the benefits of rural e-commerce can extend to local public safety.

  • The effect of government expenditure and comparative advantage on agricultural green development in OECD countries

    Qing Sun and Shinichi Kitano

    Journal of Environmental Information Science · 2026, vol. 2026, no. 1, pp. 25–36

    Abstract

    We compare two measures of agricultural productivity across OECD countries over 2005–2021. The green measure accounts for the cost of carbon emissions and the benefit of carbon stored in soil. In a dynamic panel analysis, countries with a stronger revealed comparative advantage in agriculture score higher on both measures. Government spending on agriculture is more strongly associated with conventional productivity than with green productivity.
